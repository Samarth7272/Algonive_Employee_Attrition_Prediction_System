How it's built

Four stages, one pipeline

From raw HR spreadsheet to a live risk score — each stage feeds the next, in this exact order.

01

Dataset selection

Pull publicly available HR analytics data — job role, salary band, overtime, tenure, and exit history.

02

Feature engineering

Isolate the signals that actually move the needle: satisfaction score, years since promotion, commute, workload.

03

Classification model

Logistic regression, decision trees, and random forests compete; the best F1-score wins the deployment slot.

04

Visualization

Every prediction lands on a cockpit dashboard — risk tiers, department heatmaps, and retention trendlines.
